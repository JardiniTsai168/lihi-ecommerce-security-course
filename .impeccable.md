# Design Context

## Target audience
Taiwanese ecommerce brand owners, operators, founders, and managers responsible for customer data, information security, legal compliance, or incident response.

## Use cases
- Understand why platform breaches still create responsibilities for brands.
- Evaluate the practical value of the course and its speakers.
- Review the date, format, price, agenda, organizer, and sponsors.
- Proceed to course registration when the registration URL is supplied.

## Brand personality
lihi is direct, optimistic, practical, and confident. The visual system uses official lihi pink `#FF6B9D`, deep navy `#1A1A5E`, and soft blush `#FFF5F8`. The page should feel like an editorial course launch rather than a generic cybersecurity landing page.

## Design constraints
- Preserve every piece of visible source content; redesign presentation only.
- Use the official lihi logo.
- Keep major headings to at most two lines on desktop and mobile.
- Base spacing on 30px increments where practical.
- Preserve all source links and speaker/partner assets.
- Support 320px through large desktop layouts and respect reduced motion.
